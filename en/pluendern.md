# 🚫 Plundering Rules: Trade Carts & Bounty Quests

Our server enforces a strict peace rule for daily activities: **Plundering within your own kingdom (your own server) is strictly forbidden!**

---

## 1. What Is Strictly Forbidden?
* **Trade Carts:** You must not raid or plunder Trade Carts belonging to players from your own server.
* **Bounty Quests:** You must not plunder Bounty Quests belonging to players from your own server.
* **Allowed Targets:** Only plunder Trade Carts and Bounty Quests from **foreign kingdoms (other servers)**. Always check the server number in front of the player's name before attacking!

---

## 2. Consequences for Breaking the Rules
To keep our server strong and fair for upcoming cross-server events, the following penalties apply to rule-breakers:

* **First Offense (Retaliation Strike):** The affected player has the official right to **attack the attacker's castle once** to recover their lost resources.
* **Repeated Offenses:** Anyone who repeatedly violates the no-plundering rule within our own kingdom will be **kicked from the guild**.

---

## 3. How to Prevent Accidental Plundering (Cart Filter)
To avoid accidentally attacking a Trade Cart from your own kingdom, use the built-in filter option in the game:

1. Open the **Trade Carts** menu.
2. Switch to the **Plunder / Raid** tab (where target carts are listed).
3. Check the box labeled **"Hide carts from your kingdom"** (or similar).

Once this box is checked, only carts from other servers will be displayed.

## 4. How to Plunder Bounty Quests on Other Servers
To make sure you stay on foreign servers for Bounty Quests and grab the best loot possible, follow these steps:

1. **Jump to a Foreign Server:** Open the Trade Carts menu (with the filter enabled) and tap **Plunder** on any cart from a foreign server—this will immediately take you to that other kingdom's world map.
2. **Zoom Out on the Map:** Zoom out slightly on the world map to get a better overview of active Bounty Quests in the area.
3. **Choose the Right Chest Color:** Pay attention to the color of the chests to make the most of your daily attempts:
   * 🟨 **Gold Chests:** Best loot (always prioritize)
   * 🟪 **Purple Chests:** Medium loot (solid alternative)
   * 🟦 **Turquoise / Cyan Chests:** Poor loot (avoid if possible)
4. **Check the Number:** The **number displayed on the chest** indicates how many items are inside—**the higher the number, the better the reward**.
