# ⚔️ Weekly Schedule & Guide: Alliance Duel (Monday – Saturday)

In the **Alliance Duel (VS)**, our alliance competes against an opposing alliance over 6 days (Monday through Saturday). Each day features a different theme. **The golden rule is simple: Save your resources, speedups, and tasks all week, and only use them on the specific day they score points!**

---

## 💡 Golden Rules for the Entire Week
* **Prioritize Alliance Duel Tech:** Invest your **Valor Badges** into the **"Alliance Duel"** research tree first—this dramatically increases the points you earn for every single action.
* **Don't Open Gift Boxes Early:** Wait until Tuesday to unwrap finished buildings—as long as you don't tap the completed building icon, the power points won't count yet.
* **Double-Dip Rewards:** Time your major upgrades (building, research, training) with the hourly **Race to Ace** events to get twice the rewards for the exact same effort.

---

## 📅 Daily Breakdown: Monday to Saturday

### 🟢 Day 1: Monday – Intel Training
* **Match Weight:** 1 Duel Point
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Completing & claiming **Intel Missions**
  * Spending **Stamina** (hunting monsters/butchers)
  * Gathering resources on the world map
  * Upgrading Relics / Companions (using Data & Parts)
* **Strategy:**
  * **Preparation:** Leave completed Intel Missions unclaimed on Sunday so you can start Monday with a full board right after reset.
  * Send out gathering marches on Sunday evening so they return after Monday's reset.
  * *Save all Construction Speedups for Tuesday!*

---

### 🟡 Day 2: Tuesday – Castle Expansion
* **Match Weight:** 2 Duel Points
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Using **Construction Speedups** & increasing building power
  * Dispatching **UR Trucks** (legendary trade wagons)
  * Completing **UR Bounty Tasks**
  * Upgrading decorations & decoration parts
  * Using Worker Recruitment Tickets
* **Strategy:**
  * Tap and unwrap all finished buildings (gift boxes) you saved from previous days.
  * Refresh your Trade Trucks and Bounty Tasks specifically for **UR (Gold/Orange)** rarity before starting them.
  * *Save all Research Speedups for Wednesday!*

---

### 🔵 Day 3: Wednesday – Tech Research
* **Match Weight:** 2 Duel Points
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Using **Research Speedups** & increasing tech power
  * Spending **Valor Badges** in research
  * Opening Relic / Component chests
  * Completing **Intel Missions**
* **Strategy:**
  * Focus on researching nodes in the **"Alliance Duel"** tree today, as this spends Valor Badges while permanently boosting your future duel scores.
  * *Save all Hero Shards, Tickets, and Skill Medals for Thursday!*

---

### 🟣 Day 4: Thursday – Hero Development
* **Match Weight:** 2 Duel Points
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Recruiting heroes (using Recruitment Tickets)
  * Using **Hero Shards** for star upgrades
  * Spending **Skill Medals**
  * Consuming **Hero EXP**
* **Strategy:**
  * Spend all the tickets, shards, and skill medals you saved throughout the week.
  * Focus your resources on your **main damage dealers** in your primary squad rather than spreading upgrades evenly across all heroes.
  * *Save all Training Speedups for Friday!*

---

### 🟠 Day 5: Friday – Soldier / Survivor Training
* **Match Weight:** 2 Duel Points
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Training and **Promoting** soldiers
  * Using **Training Speedups**
  * *(Depending on the weekly rotation, Construction/Research speedups & Intel tasks may also count)*
* **Strategy:**
  * If you train lower-tier troops during the week, you can **Promote** them to your highest unlocked tier on Friday using Training Speedups—this is much cheaper and gives the highest points per minute!

---

### 🔴 Day 6: Saturday – Enemy Elimination
* **Match Weight:** 4 Duel Points (often decides the entire week!)
* **What Scores Points:**
  * Defeating enemy troops (in the enemy kingdom / on the map)
  * Using **Healing Speedups**
  * Dispatching **UR Trucks** & completing **UR Bounty Tasks**
* **Strategy:**
  * **Shield Up:** Whenever you are not actively fighting or when you go offline, activate a **Peace Shield** immediately so the enemy cannot farm free points off your base!
  * Hunt enemy gatherers, hit unshielded bases, or join organized alliance rallies.
  * **Sunday (Rest Day):** Do not waste any resources! Prep your gathering marches and Intel tasks for Monday.
