# 🚢 Guide & Rules: Alliance Cargo Ship

The **Alliance Cargo Ship** is a joint alliance transport voyage where members can board the ship, submit Trade Contracts, and earn valuable rewards.

---

## 📋 Basic Rules & Timeline
* **Requirement:** Our alliance needs at least **20 members** to launch the ship.
* **Captain:** An **R4 or R5** appoints the Captain for the voyage.
* **Preparation Time:** Once preparation starts, the alliance has **4 hours** to sign up for a spot and upgrade the cargo before the ship automatically sets sail.

---

## 🛏️ Seats & Cabins (How Many Can Join?)
* **4 Cabins:** The ship features **4 different cabins**, each offering different rewards.
* **Seats per Cabin:** By default, **5 players per cabin** can board the ship (for a total of **20 passengers** + the Captain).
* **More Seats via Upgrades:** Upgrading the ship through Alliance Gifts / Alliance Progress increases the passenger capacity of the cabins.

---

## 🎟️ How Selection Works (Boarding)
1. **Submit Trade Contracts:** To apply for a spot in a cabin, you must submit **Trade Contracts** (the default requirement is **3 contracts**).
2. **Priority, Not a Guarantee:** Getting a seat is **not guaranteed**. The more Trade Contracts you submit, the higher your priority will be when seats are assigned.
3. **Refunds if Not Selected:** If you are not selected when the ship departs, your submitted **Trade Contracts are refunded via mail**.
   * *Warning about Gems:* Missing contracts can be substituted with Gems. However, if you don't make it onto the ship, you will be refunded in **Trade Contracts**—**not Gems**!

---

## 🏴‍☠️ Plundering & Tips for Everyone
* **Captain's Cabin is Safe:** The Captain's cargo **cannot** be plundered.
* **Passenger Cabins:** Other alliances can raid the ship during its voyage (up to **3 times per trip**; each raid steals up to 3 pieces of cargo from 3 players).
* **Support the Captain:** As a passenger, you can use the plus (+) icon to give Tickets/Likes to the Captain. The Captain uses these tickets to refresh the ship's cargo for better rewards.
