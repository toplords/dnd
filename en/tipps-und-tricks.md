# 💡 Tips & Tricks for Top Lords

Here are the best daily tricks to grab free Diamonds, save resources, and maximize your points during the Alliance Duel:

---

## 💎 Free Diamonds & Daily Must-Dos
* **Like the Top 3 (3× 30 Diamonds):** Open the **Alliance Duel (VS)** ranking/podium (or the Arena podium) and give a "Like" to the top 3 players every day for **90 free Diamonds**.
* **Daily Free Chests:** Don't forget to claim your free daily points and chests in the Shop and VIP menu.

---

## ⚔️ Combat & Scouting Tricks
* **Always Scout Before Attacking:** Send out a scout before launching any attack:
  * **Against Bosses & Monsters:** Scouting grants a **+1% Damage Buff**, allowing your troops to deal extra damage.
  * **Against Enemy Players:** You can see the exact troop count, reinforcements, and resources inside their base beforehand to avoid unnecessary losses.
* **Heal Troops in Small Batches:** After heavy fights, don't heal all wounded soldiers at once. Heal them in **small batches of 30 to 45 minutes** so alliance members can speed up each batch for free using the "Help" button—saving almost all your Healing Speedups.

---

## ⚡ Resource-Saving & Event Tricks
* **Heal Troops in Small Batches:** After heavy fights, don't heal all wounded soldiers at once. Heal them in **small batches of 30 to 45 minutes** so alliance members can speed up each batch for free using the "Help" button—saving almost all your Healing Speedups.
* **Promote Lower-Tier Troops:** Train cheaper, lower-tier troops during the week and **Promote** them to your highest tier on **Friday (Alliance Duel)**. This costs fewer resources and gives massive event points in very little time.
* **Pre-Farm Intel Missions:** Complete your final Intel Missions on **Sunday**, but **do not claim them**. Collect them right after Monday's reset for an instant head start on Day 1 points.
* **Time Your Gathering Marches:** Send out your gathering squads on **Sunday evening** so they return **after Monday's reset**—all gathered resources will immediately count toward Monday's score.

---

## 🛡️ Defense & Alliance Benefits
* **Keep Resource Boxes in Your Bag:** Only open resource items from your inventory right when you are about to spend them. Unopened boxes in your bag **cannot be plundered** if your base gets attacked.
* **Saturday Protection (Enemy Elimination):** Put up a **Peace Shield** on Saturday whenever you go offline. If you are out of shields, send your troops far away to gather or garrison protected alliance buildings so the enemy can't farm free kill points off your base.
* **Donate to Alliance Tech:** Donate gold regularly to the recommended Alliance Tech—this buffs the entire alliance and earns you currency for the **Alliance Shop** (essential for Teleports and Peace Shields).
