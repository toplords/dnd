# 🏹 Guide & Rules: Wild Hunt

The **Wild Hunt** is a daily solo event where you hunt wild animals on the map and collect valuable resources.

---

## 📋 Basic Rules & Format
* **Daily Rounds:** The event runs in daily cycles with multiple rounds.
* **Wild Animals:** Various wild animals spawn on the map that need to be hunted.
* **Time Limit:** Each round has a time limit - collect as many resources as possible!

---

## 🏆 How Points Are Scored

### 1. Alliance Points (Determines the Winner)
* **Hunting Animals:** Each hunted animal grants alliance points.
* **Rare Animals:** Rare or legendary animals grant significantly more points.
* **Cooperation:** Joint hunting of large animals grants additional points.

### 2. Personal Points (For Individual Rewards)
* **Resource Collection:** Each hunted animal drops various resources.
* **Combos:** Serial hunting in quick succession grants multiplier bonuses.
* **Objective Completion:** Completing specific hunting objectives grants additional rewards.

---

## 🎯 The 3 Golden Rules for Our Team
1. **Coordinate your hunt:** All players hunt on different sectors to cover as many animals as possible.
2. **Prioritize rare animals:** Rare and legendary animals have the highest point value - divide sectors so everyone can hunt rare animals.
3. **Don't neglect resources:** Besides points, you also collect valuable resources - use the hunting time optimally!
