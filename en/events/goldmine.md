# ⛏️ Complete Guide & Rules: Gold Mine Event

The **Gold Mine Event** (*Gold Mine Conquest*) is a 30-minute real-time alliance battlefield where two alliances compete head-to-head. The goal is to have more **Alliance Points** than the opponent when the timer runs out.

---

## 📋 1. Basic Rules, Participation & Troop Mechanics

* **Participants:** Up to **20 active players** and **10 substitutes** per alliance (registration is handled by R4/R5; eligible members must meet the required Castle level and alliance membership criteria).
* **Duration:** **30 minutes** of real-time combat on a dedicated event map.
* **Victory Condition:** The alliance with the highest total points when the 30 minutes end wins.
* **Dedicated Event Troop Pool:** You fight with a separate event troop pool (based on your unlocked troop tiers). Your real troops back home remain **completely safe** and cannot be killed or plundered.
* **No Permanent Deaths:** Soldiers do not die permanently during the event. Wounded troops go to the wounded tent and are automatically restored after the event ends.
* **Safe Zone:** Your starting safe zone is protected. Once you teleport out onto the battlefield, you cannot return to the safe zone.

---

## 🏥 2. How Does Healing in the Medical Camp / Hospital Work?

* **Do you have to capture a Medical Camp building first to heal?**
  **No!** You can heal at any time, even if your alliance does not currently hold a *Medical Camp* on the map.
* **Manual Collection (Important!):** Healed soldiers do **not** return to your army automatically. Tap the **Medical / Hospital icon** in the bottom-left corner regularly to collect your healed troops so they are ready to deploy again.
* **Bonus Effect from Medical Camp Buildings:** Whenever our alliance holds a *Medical Camp* on the map, it automatically heals **15 soldiers of any tier every 10 seconds**—completely free and without using speedups. The more Medical Camps we hold, the faster our alliance's army regenerates!

## ⏱️ 4. Event Flow in 3 Phases

### Phase 1: Minutes 0 to 10 (Secure Territory & Build Buffs)
* **Primary Objectives:** Capture the **2 Smelteries** (highest points in Phase 1), the **Intel Post** (+10% points on all held buildings), and the **Scholar Workshop** (faster teleports).
* **Secondary Objectives:** Occupy as many of the **4 Medical Camps** as possible to earn a steady 30 points/sec each and keep troops healing.

### Phase 2: From Minute 10 (Center Clash & Combat Buffs)
* **Unlocks:** The **Gold Mine** (80 pts/sec), **Weapon Forge** (+15% own hero stats), and **Tower of Judgment** (-15% enemy hero stats) open up in the center area.
* **Strategy:** Our strongest players and rallies focus on the Gold Mine and the two combat-buff buildings. Holding the Weapon Forge and Tower of Judgment gives a major advantage in winning the Gold Mine.

### Phase 3: Final Phase (Hold, Collect Crates & Quarries)
* **Unlocks:** **Quarries** spawn across the map.
* **Strategy:** Defend held point buildings until the timer runs out. Meanwhile, send available or lighter marches into Quarries to finish personal point milestones.

---

## 🏆 5. How Are Points Earned?

### 1. Alliance Points (Crucial for Victory!)
* **Buildings Only:** Alliance Points are generated **exclusively** by occupying and holding buildings (boosted by the *Intel Post*).
* **Pure Kills Don't Count:** Defeating enemy troops in the open field or at castles awards **0 Alliance Points**. Fighting only matters to take or defend buildings.

### 2. Personal Points (For Your Individual Rewards)
* **Occupying Buildings:** You earn ongoing personal points while your troops occupy a building (up to a cap of roughly 54,000 points).
* **Defeating Enemies:** Battles against enemy players count toward your personal points.
* **Collecting Gold Packets / Crates:** Every time a building changes ownership ("flip"), crates scatter around it. **Anyone** can collect them—whether we just captured or lost the building.
* **Gathering Quarries (Phase 3):** In the final stretch of the event, gather from Quarries for additional personal points (5 points per gathering second).

---

## 🎯 6. The 5 Golden Rules for Our Team

1. **Buildings Over Pointless Fights:** Do not chase random enemy castles or lone marches. Points come from buildings!
2. **Strong Marches in Key Buildings:** Our strongest players hold Smelteries, the Gold Mine, the Weapon Forge, and the Tower of Judgment. Newer or lighter players help with Medical Camps, crates, and Quarries.
3. **Grab Crates Immediately:** As soon as a building flips, send spare/fast marches to pick up the surrounding crates before enemies claim them.
4. **Don't Forget the Hospital Icon:** Check the hospital icon in the bottom-left corner frequently and collect healed troops so you don't run out of fighting power.
5. **Follow Chat Callouts:** Avoid solo attacks on random targets; coordinate teleports and rallies on called objectives.
