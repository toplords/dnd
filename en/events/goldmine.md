# ⛏️ Guide & Rules: Gold Mine Conquest Event

The **Gold Mine Conquest** is a 30-minute alliance battlefield event where two alliances compete against each other. The goal is to have more **Alliance Points** than the opponent when the timer runs out.

---

## 📋 Basic Rules & Format
* **Participants:** Up to **20 active players** and **10 substitutes** per alliance.
* **Duration:** **30 minutes** of real-time combat on a dedicated event map.
* **Win Condition:** The alliance with the highest total points at the end of the 30 minutes wins.

---

## 🏆 How Points Are Scored

### 1. Alliance Points (Determines the Winner)
* **Holding Buildings:** Alliance points are generated **exclusively** by capturing and holding buildings on the map.
* **Troop Power Matters:** The stronger the troops stationed inside a building, the more points are generated.
* *Important:* Simply defeating enemy troops does **not** grant Alliance Points!

### 2. Personal Points (For Individual Rewards)
* **Occupying Buildings:** Generates continuous personal points (up to a cap of around 54,000 points).
* **Defeating Enemies:** Winning battles against enemy players adds to your personal score.
* **Collecting Gold Packages / Scatter Boxes:** Every time a building changes ownership ("flips"), boxes scatter around that building. **Anyone** can collect them (even from buildings your own alliance just lost).
* **Quarries (Phase 3):** In the final stage of the match, gathering nodes spawn on the map where you can mine for extra personal points.

---

## 🎯 The 3 Golden Rules for Our Team
1. **Strongest Troops in Buildings:** Our strongest players must garrison and defend key buildings. Do not chase random fights across the map.
2. **Fast Marches for Boxes:** As soon as any building flips, send out fast marches immediately to grab the scattered gold packages before the enemy takes them.
3. **Focus on Assigned Targets:** Do not split up randomly—attack and defend the specific buildings called out by leadership.
