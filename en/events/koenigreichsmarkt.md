# 🏪 Guide & Rules: Kingdom Market

The **Kingdom Market** is a daily event where you trade your resources and acquire valuable items.

---

## 📋 Basic Rules & Format
* **Daily Rounds:** The market runs in daily cycles with multiple rounds.
* **Trading Posts:** At various trading posts, you can exchange resources.
* **Changing Prices:** Trading prices change daily and offer different opportunities.

---

## 🏆 How Points Are Scored

### 1. Alliance Points (Determines the Winner)
* **Completing Trades:** Each completed trade grants alliance points.
* **Large Transactions:** High-value trades grant significantly more points.
* **Market Dominance:** Controlling certain trading posts generates continuous points.

### 2. Personal Points (For Individual Rewards)
* **Trade Volume:** The higher the trade volume, the more personal points.
* **Trade Bonuses:** Completing specific trade objectives grants additional rewards.
* **Rare Goods:** Trading rare goods grants multiplier bonuses.

---

## 🎯 The 3 Golden Rules for Our Team
1. **Coordinated trading:** All players trade at different posts to cover the entire market.
2. **Take advantage of good prices:** Pay attention to the daily prices - trade preferably when prices are favorable.
3. **Plan your resources:** Collect resources in advance to be able to trade immediately at the best opportunities!
