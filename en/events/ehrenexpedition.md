# Expeditions (Tribe, Legion & Ranger)

In **Top Lords**, **Expeditions** (*Tribe*, *Legion*, and *Ranger*) are special challenges where you fight your way up floor by floor against increasingly stronger enemies to earn Expedition Points and valuable resources.

---

## 1. How to Access Expeditions (Inconspicuous Tower)
You can access the mode directly from your city view:
1. **Find the Inconspicuous Tower:** Look around your city area for the **Inconspicuous Tower** (located slightly off to the side of your main buildings).
2. **Open Expeditions:** Tap on the tower to open the Expedition menu.
3. **Choose a Path:** Select one of the three paths—**Tribe**, **Legion**, or **Ranger** (depending on which paths are open on that day).

## 2. Expedition Shop Rewards
As you progress, you earn **Expedition Points** that can be exchanged for key upgrades in the **Expedition Shop**:

| Shop Item | Priority | Benefit & Recommendation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Expedition Chests** | **1 (Must-Buy)** | Offers a chance at **100 Universal UR Hero Shards**, Skill Medals, Gems, and Speedups. Buy the weekly maximum (10 chests) every week. |
| **Decor Pieces** | **2 (Very High)** | Unlocks permanent stat boosts (Attack, Defense, HP) across your entire account. |
| **Worker Tokens** | **3 (High)** | Lets you buy Worker fragments to speed up construction and research times. |
| **Resource & Speedup Chests** | **4 (Optional)** | Worth picking up if you have already bought the top priorities and have leftover points. |
| **Beast Meat & Basic Tickets** | **Avoid** | Not worth spending points on, as you can easily farm these items from regular world map activities. |
