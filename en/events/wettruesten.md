# ⏱️ Guide & Rules: Race to Ace (Arms Race)

**Race to Ace** is a daily solo event where you can earn valuable speedups, skill medals, and resources simply by timing your regular upgrades (building, research, heroes, troops, and griffin).

---

## 📋 Basic Rules & Schedule
* **6 Rounds per Day:** Each day is divided into **6 rounds lasting 4 hours each**.
* **5 Task Themes:** There are 5 rotating task categories in total:
  1. **Castle Building** (Construction speedups & building power)
  2. **Tech Research** (Research speedups & tech power)
  3. **Hero Growth** (Recruitment tickets, hero shards, skill medals)
  4. **Soldier Training** (Training/promoting troops & training speedups)
  5. **Griffin Upgrades** (Spending stamina & upgrading your Griffin)
* **Changing Order:** Because there are 6 rounds in a day and 5 themes, **one theme repeats twice each day**—and the schedule changes daily.

---

## 🎯 Optimal Strategy (Double-Dip Rewards!)
1. **Always Check the Event Calendar First:** Tap the **calendar icon** near the top of the Race to Ace screen to see the exact schedule for the day (in server time!). Never spend speedups without checking which round is active first.
2. **Sync with the Alliance Duel (VS):** Race to Ace is the ultimate multiplier for the Alliance Duel. For example, on **Tuesday (Castle Expansion in VS)**, wait until the 4-hour **Castle Building** window in Race to Ace opens before using your construction speedups—this earns you **double rewards** for the exact same resources.
3. **Aim for Milestone Chests:** Each 4-hour round offers 3 reward chests. Push for the chests that match that day's Alliance Duel theme, but save your remaining resources for upcoming VS days.
4. **Ignore Pack Tasks (F2P):** Every round includes one task for buying packs—if you are Free-to-Play, ignore that task completely and focus purely on the free upgrade objectives.
