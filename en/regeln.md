# 🤝 What Does NAP10 / NAP15 Mean?

**NAP** stands for **Non-Aggression Pact**. The number next to it (e.g., **NAP10** or **NAP15**) indicates how many of the strongest alliances on a server are covered by this peace agreement.

* **NAP10:** The **top 10 alliances** (based on Alliance Power in the server rankings) do not attack each other.
* **NAP15:** The agreement applies to the **top 15 alliances** on the server.

---

## 1. Why Does a Server Have a NAP?
A NAP is usually established by the strongest alliances on a server to prevent chaos and keep the entire server strong for cross-server events (such as **KvK / Server vs. Server**):

* **Safe Farming & Growth:** Members of protected alliances don't have to constantly worry about their castles being plundered overnight or their troops being wiped out.
* **Stronger Server in KvK:** When top alliances don't weaken each other, far more troops and resources remain available for server wars.
* **Clear Rules of Play:** It creates a stable environment where players can focus on events, monster hunts, and upgrading their base.

---

## 2. Typical Rules in a NAP10 / NAP15
While each server sets its own specific terms, these core rules apply almost everywhere:

* **No Attacks on Castles & Gathering Nodes:** Castles, outposts, and resource tiles belonging to members of NAP alliances must not be attacked or scouted.
* **No Plundering Within Your Own Kingdom (Trade Carts & Bounty Quests):** Plundering **Trade Carts** and **Bounty Quests** belonging to other players from your own kingdom (especially NAP alliances) is **strictly forbidden**. Only target players from foreign servers/kingdoms when plundering.
* **Do Not Destroy Alliance Territory:** Borders and banners of other NAP alliances must be respected.
* **Special Rules During Kill Events:** During certain PvP events (such as *Kill Event* or *Top Lord*), the NAP may either be temporarily lifted or restricted to gathering-tile battles only (no castle hits).
* **Penalties for Rule-Breakers:** If a player attacks a NAP member or plunders within their own kingdom, they must usually pay compensation resources or risk being kicked from their alliance so the entire alliance doesn't lose its NAP protection.

---

## 3. Important Tips for You as a Player
* **Keep an Eye on Alliance Rankings:** If your alliance is ranked 10th or 15th, dropping to 11th or 16th can mean temporarily losing NAP protection.
* **Check the Server Number Before Plundering:** Always verify that the target's server number on Trade Carts and Bounty Quests is **not** your own kingdom number.
* **Read Alliance Announcements:** Check alliance mail or your server Discord regularly, as specific exceptions (such as weekend event rules) are posted there.
