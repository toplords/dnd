# 👑 Kingdom Market

The **Kingdom Market** is a limited-time event in **Top Lords** that returns every few weeks. Here, you can claim free Diamonds every day and spend a small amount of Diamonds to collect valuable Crowns for the event shop.

---

## 1. Claim Daily Free Diamonds & Crowns
Visit the Kingdom Market every day while the event is active to grab these two offers:

* **100 Free Diamonds:** Claim your **100 free Diamonds** directly inside the event menu.
* **60 Crowns for 500 Diamonds:** Every day, you can purchase a bundle of **60 Crowns** for **500 Diamonds**. Since you receive 100 Diamonds for free, the 60 Crowns effectively only cost 400 Diamonds—offering great value.

---

## 2. Exchange Crowns in the Event Shop
You can trade your collected **Crowns** directly in the Kingdom Market Shop for rare and valuable resources (make sure to spend all your Crowns before the event ends):

| Shop Item | Priority | Benefit & Recommendation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Universal UR Hero Shards & Choice Chests** | **1 (Must-Buy)** | The best investment for your Crowns to specifically upgrade your strongest UR heroes. |
| **Gear Drafts (UR / MR)** | **2 (Very High)** | Provides a massive combat power boost for your main heroes. |
| **Rare Resources & Materials (e.g., Pig Iron, Ore)** | **3 (High)** | Ideal for overcoming bottlenecks in gear and account progression. |
| **Permanent Castle Skins** | **4 (Long-Term)** | Offer permanent stat bonuses, though they usually require significantly more Crowns or paid packs. |
