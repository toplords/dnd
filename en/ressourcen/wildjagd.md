# The "Wild Hunt" Event

In **Top Lords**, the **Wild Hunt** is a limited-time event (found in the Hot Deals / Events section) where you use hunting arrows on a game board to take down animals, collect Hunter Tokens, and exchange them for valuable rewards.

---

## 1. How to Play Wild Hunt & Get Arrows
Various animals (such as falcons and wild boars) appear on the event board for you to shoot with your arrows:

1. **Claim 10 Free Arrows Daily:** You receive **10 free hunting arrows every day** directly in the event menu. Make sure to claim and use them daily.
2. **Extra Arrows for Gems:** Optionally, you can buy **20 additional arrows for 500 Gems** once per day to clear more targets and unlock higher milestone chests.
3. **Hunt Animals & Collect Hunter Tokens:** Every hit and defeated animal rewards you with **Hunter Tokens** as well as progress toward event milestones.

## 2. Event Shop Rewards
Exchange your collected **Hunter Tokens** directly in the **Wild Hunt Shop** for key upgrades (make sure to spend all tokens before the event ends, as they expire afterward):

| Shop Item | Priority | Benefit & Recommendation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Universal UR Hero Shards** | **1 (Must-Buy)** | The most valuable reward in the event—ideal for upgrading your strongest heroes. |
| **Beast Cores** | **2 (Very High)** | Essential for upgrading your Beasts and boosting their combat power. |
| **Decor Pieces & Decor Chests** | **3 (High)** | Unlocks permanent stat boosts (Attack, Defense, HP) across your entire account. |
| **Speedups & Skill Medals** | **4 (Optional)** | A solid choice for spending any leftover Hunter Tokens right before the event ends. |
| **Basic Resources & Beast Meat** | **Avoid** | Not worth spending tokens on, as you can easily farm these items for free on the world map. |
