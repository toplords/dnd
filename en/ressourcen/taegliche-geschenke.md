# 🎁 Daily Gifts

Here are the most important daily tricks to collect free Diamonds and resources:

---

## 💎 VS Duel Arena
* **Like the Top 3 (3× 30 Diamonds):** Open the ranking/podium in the **Alliance Duel (VS)** (or Arena podium) and give a daily "Like" to the top 3 players—this instantly grants you **90 free Diamonds** per day.
* **Battle Alliance Members:** Complete battles to claim the rewards from the three chests.
